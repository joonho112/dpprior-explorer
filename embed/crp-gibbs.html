A collapsed Gibbs sampler for the CRP mixture

One point at a time, a new cluster is chosen with probability proportional to the CRP prior weight times the fit. Start from one cluster, singletons or a random split; move alpha live. Enter resamples one point. Use ?preset=one|singletons|random, ?alpha=, ?n= (sweeps).
