DP or not: finite Dirichlet weights against stick-breaking

A finite Dirichlet mixture never uses more than its K components, while stick-breaking keeps supplying new ones: K_N stops at 5 or 50 but keeps growing like alpha log N for the Dirichlet process. ?preset=scaled gives the finite models the same total mass alpha. Enter redraws.
