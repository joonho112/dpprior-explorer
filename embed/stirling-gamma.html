Stirling-gamma vs Gamma(2, 4): a pinned count, moving weights

Sg(4, 1, n) and Sg(1, 0.25, n) keep E K_n = 4 at every n, where Gamma(2, 4) drifts like log n (3.20 at 100, 4.35 at 1,000, 5.51 at 10,000). The weights, not examined by the Sg papers, move instead: Pr(W_SB > .5) rises from about .3 to .77 by n = 2000. Use ?n=.
