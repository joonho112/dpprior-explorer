DORO: a flat count target the Gamma cannot reach

Move n: the bars are the count distribution of the Gamma(a, b) closest in KL divergence to a flat target on 1 to n (dashed), solved live. The gap grows with n, b goes to 0, and E K_n follows n / 2 while a fixed Gamma grows like log n. Switch to DORO-Unif for a flat target on 1 to 2 mu_K - 1 at J = 100. Use ?n= or ?preset= with doro, unif5, unif10 or unif30.
